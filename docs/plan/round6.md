## Why round 6

Round 5 found that stock VPR packing leaves about 7% CLB, 22% WL and 9% CPD against the known clustering on mesh and tree
(chain and random about 1.00), peaking near B = 64. The Norns HDL-aware packer gained (dp WL 0.834) only through a LEAK: in
round-5 `dp` designs the HDL word file equals the block partition (word-file purity 0.9998), and on `ctl` designs (no words) its
`.net` equalled stock (27 of 27). Round 5 therefore cannot say whether HDL knowledge helps when the HDL does not hand over
the answer. Round 6 changes only one thing, how much the HDL words agree with the blocks (alignment a), and keeps the logic,
the topologies and the known optimum of round 5.

**Research question (RQ-6).** As the alignment a between the HDL words and the optimal blocks falls from 1 (the leak) to 0 (words
that share nothing with the blocks), how does the gain of the Norns HDL-aware packer over the stock VPR packer in WL, CLB count
and CPD change, at what a, if any, does it stop beating stock, and does it ever do worse than stock? The one-sentence answer
the paper must give is built from H33 to H36 below.

## Common definitions

* Architecture, flow, VPR flags, Odin III script, heavy-lock rule, seeds, cut, block labels, purity against the known
  blocks, exact block, null purity, ratio per design, statistics (Wilcoxon, Holm, bootstrap with seed 20261026 and 10000
  resamples, status words, "needs n" rule): exactly as `docs/plan/round5.md` "Common definitions" and Stage 2, with these
  readings. The unit is the design. Per-design value of a ratio X/Y of a metric = geometric mean over the VPR seeds (1, 2, 3)
  of m_X(seed) / m_Y(seed) over seeds where both runs have rc 0; a design needs >= 2 matched seeds, else it is dropped from that
  test (and listed). A rule needs n >= 12 designs (>= 8 cells for H36), else the status is "inconclusive (n too small)".
* Word file: the file `norns` is fed, built exactly as round 4/5 (`affinity.write(design, "word", ...)`, Odin III
  provenance word recovery on the generated Verilog), written once per design as the HDL is written, never edited, never
  derived from the block labels. Word-file purity (the MEASURED alignment `a_m` of a design): over the FFs that carry a word and
  a block label, sum over words of the largest number of FFs of one block in the word, divided by the number of such FFs.
  The designed alignment `a` is the level (0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1) ; the layout also gives a designed purity, reported.
* Primary metric of every Norns/stock hypothesis: WL (routed wirelength). CLB count (`logic_clbs`) and CPD
  (`Final critical path delay`) are secondary: each hypothesis gives the status they get under the same rule, with Holm over
  its own family, and the paper prints all three; the hypothesis status is the WL status. The status of a CLB or CPD is never
  used to rescue or overturn the WL status.
* Placebo (the null for every "found" claim): for each cell (topology, B) the ratio stock(level a) / stock(level 1) of the same
  cell, which compares two synthesis outputs of the SAME logic whose HDL differs only in register names, so the BLIF names and
  orders differ; its geometric mean and 90% bootstrap interval (over the cell's designs) are printed beside each Norns/stock
  geometric mean. The paper calls a Norns effect "found" only if its status is supported (or, for a harm claim, supported) AND
  its geometric mean lies outside the placebo 90% interval of the same level. This is a wording rule; it changes no status.
* Status words: supported / not supported / inconclusive; the literal rule on the n actually available is the status;
  variants are labelled and change no status; every post-data analysis is a dated exploratory entry at the end of this file.

## Stage 1: the generator (`gen/r6gen.py`)

Deterministic from the integer 20261026 and the design's parameters (Python `random.Random`, no global state, pure
Python). Output: one Verilog file per design, a spec JSON and `data/r6/designs.json` (parameters, layout statistics, designed
cut, SHA-256 of the Verilog).

* Logic. The block structure, topology, functions, external wiring and fixed I/O of a round-6 design are exactly those of the
  round-5 `dp` design of the same topology and size (`r5gen.build_spec("r5_<topology>_dp_<B>")`): 10 flip-flops
  per block, each fed by a 6-input function (one 6-LUT), a block fills exactly one CLB, designed cut as round 5. The known
  optimal clustering is the block partition (block `b<k>` = instance `u<k>`, module `b<k>`), identical to round 5.
* What changes is only the declaration of the registers in the HDL. Inside every block the ten registers are the scalars `r0..r9`
  and the ten output ports are scalars `q0..q9` (no bus, so Odin III finds no bus name inside a block). The top module
  declares B vectors `wire [9:0] w<j>`; flip-flop (k, i) drives bit `bit` of word `w<j>` (the instance port `.q<i>(w<j>[bit])`).
  The HDL word set is therefore {w0 .. w(B-1)}, each of ten flip-flops. LUTs carry no word (as round 5 `dp`). The
  `po` output bits are the same flip-flops; their word is the vector `w<j>` (probe: no `po` bus word appears).
* Alignment a in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1} (level L = 100 a in the name). Layout: per block, q_k = round((1-a) 10 (k+1)) -
  round((1-a) 10 k) flip-flops are DISPLACED (error-diffusion rounding so that every block displaces the same number, up to one;
  which local indices is a seeded draw); non-displaced flip-flops stay in their own slot (word k, bit i); the displaced
  flip-flops are listed by (local index i, block k) and dealt in that order into the displaced slots listed by (word, bit).
  So a displaced flip-flop leaves its block's word, and word j then holds the same local bit of consecutive blocks (a
  bit-slice, the way a real datapath word spans blocks). Both directions are in the design: words span blocks (a=0:
  10 blocks per word) and blocks mix words (a=0: the ten flip-flops of a block sit in ten words). a=1 is the round-5 `dp`
  layout (word = block): the reference and the leak. Designed purity at a=0 is 0.100 (every word has ten flip-flops of ten
  blocks), about 0.25, 0.51, 0.76 at a = 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 (printed per design in `designs.json`).
* Why this rule and not another. (i) A word offset of (1-a) of a block cannot go below purity 0.5 and mixes only neighbours; the
  bit-slice deal goes to purity 0.1 and mixes both ways. (ii) A random reassignment would put the flip-flops of a word in
  arbitrary far blocks (unlike HDL, where a word spans adjacent slices); the deal keeps words local. (iii) Because the logic
  is unchanged and only names move, the stock and known arms of different levels of one cell run the same logic, which gives
  the placebo above.
* Topologies and sizes: mesh, tree (where round 5 found headroom) and chain (the no-headroom control, round 5 ratio about 1.00);
  `rand` is not used (round 5 ratio about 1.00 as chain); B in {16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024} (the round-5 ladder from 16;
  B = 4, 8 are below the word length 10 and cannot take a=0 with ten blocks per word). 3 topologies x 5 levels x 7 sizes = 105
  designs. Names `r6_<topology>_a<L>_<B>`. A cell is (topology, B); a level group is (topology, L).
* Synthesis keep check (the round-5 rule as amended, fixed here before round 6, plus a word-coverage item). A design is KEPT iff
  (k1) LUTs = 10 B and FFs = 10 B; (k2) every block <= 10 LUTs, <= 10 FFs, <= 40 distinct input nets; (k3) >= 99% of LUT and FF
  atoms carry a block label (path component `b<k>`, or, for a flip-flop Odin names after a primary output `po[p]`, the designed
  block of that output, as round-5 amendment 1); (k4) |measured cut - designed cut| <= 10% of the designed cut; (k5) >= 90% of
  the flip-flops carry a word in the word file. A design that is not KEPT is reported with the failing items and EXCLUDED from
  every hypothesis except H29. The known cut used in the tables is the measured cut of the blocks.

## Stage 2: measurement

Arms, 3 VPR seeds (1, 2, 3) each, heavy lock per run, one VPR at a time, smallest B first, then topology order mesh, tree,
chain, then level 0, 25, 50, 75, 100:
* `stock`: stock `vpr`.
* `norns`: the Norns packer, round-4/5 headline configuration (affinity source `word`, beta 4, `--norns_proposal_count queued`),
  fed the word file as written (not the blocks).
* `known`: the same Norns binary, an affinity file giving every labelled atom its block's id, `--norns_affinity_weight 100`,
  `--norns_proposal_count queued` (round 5). Reproduction rate = exact blocks / B; contingency K2 of round 5 stands unchanged
  (a KEPT design with reproduction < 1.0 is re-run once with `--pack`; reproduction < 0.9 excludes the design from H32 to H36,
  and counts as a failure in H31).
Run count: 105 x 3 x 3 = 945 runs. Metrics per run as round 5 (logic CLBs and gap to B, grid, purity against the blocks and
the null, exact blocks, measured cut, routed WL, W, CPD, pack and flow time), plus per design: the word-file purity `a_m`, the
share of word-file atoms in each block and the number of words. Not repeated in round 6 (stated limit): the Odin III
attribution cross-check of CPD (round 5 found VPR and attribution agree); CPD is VPR's `Final critical path delay`.
Packing does not depend on the VPR seed (round 4), so the three `.net` files of a (design, arm) are compared by SHA-256.

Time estimate (made before any round-6 run, from round-5 `flow_s` and from the probe): flow time per run is 12 s at B = 1024
(stock 15 s on mesh), 6 s at 512, 3 s at 256, 2 s at 128, 1 s at 64 and under 1 s below, sum over the seven sizes about 31
s per (topology, level, arm, seed) including stock; 15 (topology, level) x 9 runs x 31 s about 70 min of VPR. Synthesis plus
word recovery measured on the probe: 44 s at B = 256, taken as linear in B (about 3 min at 1024): about 15 x 11 min = 90 min.
Total about 3 h, under the 8 h limit, so no size is cut. If the measured time of the first 5 sizes extrapolates above 8 h in
total, the two largest sizes are cut, the cut is committed, and the paper says so.

## Hypotheses and decision rules

Ratios are arm / reference of the same design, per design as defined above. "Mesh and tree" is the set of designs with
topology mesh or tree (the two topologies where round 5 found a gap; 70 designs, 14 per level, 14 cells); chain is the control.

H29 (the construction survives synthesis), all 105 designs. Supported iff >= 90% KEPT and every level group (topology x level,
15 groups of 7 sizes) has >= 5 KEPT; not supported iff < 70% KEPT; otherwise inconclusive.

H30 (the alignment manipulation works, measured not assumed), KEPT designs. Spearman rho between the design level and the
measured word-file purity `a_m`; medians of `a_m` at level 0 and level 100. Supported iff rho >= 0.9 and median `a_m` at level 100
>= 0.95 and median `a_m` at level 0 <= 0.35. Not supported iff rho < 0.7 or median `a_m` at level 0 > 0.60 or at level 100 < 0.80.
Otherwise inconclusive. (Reachable: ABC or the word finder could merge or split vectors; the probe suggests supported, which is
a check of the construction, not a finding.)

H31 (the known arm reproduces the blocks), KEPT designs: as round-5 H24. Supported iff the reproduction rate is 1.0 on >= 90% of
KEPT designs and the mean is >= 0.99; not supported iff the mean is < 0.90; otherwise inconclusive.

H32 (there is room to gain: stock against the known clustering in this layout), mesh and tree. The unit is the cell, because the
five levels of a cell run the same logic (pseudo-replication otherwise): per cell the geometric mean over the cell's KEPT
levels of stock/known; metrics CLB, WL, CPD, Holm over the 3, one-sided (ratio > 1) Wilcoxon over the 14 cells. Supported iff the
geometric mean >= 1.05 and the Holm p < 0.05; not supported iff the geometric mean <= 1.02 or the 90% bootstrap interval (over
cells) lies inside [0.98, 1.02]; otherwise inconclusive. Status = WL status. (Round 5 saw WL 1.22 here, so supported is expected.)

H33 (the reference: with the leak, a = 1), mesh and tree designs at level 100, norns/stock. Supported iff geometric mean WL
ratio <= 0.95 and the Holm p (ratio < 1, over the 3 metrics) < 0.05; not supported iff the geometric mean >= 0.99 or the 90%
bootstrap interval lies inside [0.98, 1.02]; otherwise inconclusive. n = 14.

H34 (does HDL-aware packing beat stock without the leak?), one hypothesis per level a in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75} (H34a to H34d),
mesh and tree designs of that level, norns/stock, n = 14 each. Supported iff geometric mean WL ratio <= 0.99 and the one-sided
(ratio < 1) Wilcoxon p after Holm over the FOUR levels (WL) < 0.05; not supported iff the geometric mean >= 1.00 or the
90% interval lies inside [0.98, 1.02]; otherwise inconclusive. (CLB and CPD get the same rule with Holm over their own four
levels, secondary.) The break-even alignment a* = the lowest level in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75} with H34 supported (reported
"none" if no level is supported; the lowest measured `a_m` of a supported level is reported too).

H35 (does it ever do worse than stock?), one hypothesis per level a in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1} (H35a to H35e), mesh and tree designs,
norns/stock, n = 14 each. Supported (it hurts) iff geometric mean WL ratio >= 1.02 and the one-sided (ratio > 1) Wilcoxon p after
Holm over the FIVE levels < 0.05; not supported iff the geometric mean <= 1.01 or the 90% interval lies inside [0.98, 1.02];
otherwise inconclusive.

H36 (dose-response of the Norns gain on the measured alignment), mesh and tree cells (14). Per cell: the points (a_m,
ln(WL norns/stock)) of the cell's KEPT levels; a cell needs >= 4 levels and a spread of `a_m` >= 0.4, else it is dropped (listed);
the cell's slope is the least-squares slope of ln ratio on `a_m` (negative = more alignment, more gain). Statistic = median
slope. Metrics WL (primary), CLB, CPD, Holm over the 3, one-sided (slope < 0) Wilcoxon over the cells. Supported iff median slope
<= -0.10 (the WL ratio falls by 10% or more from a_m = 0 to 1) and slope < 0 in >= 70% of the cells and Holm p < 0.05; not supported
iff the median slope >= -0.03; otherwise inconclusive; < 8 cells = inconclusive (n too small). Reported with a 90% bootstrap
interval over cells and, as a labelled variant, the same slope on the leak-free levels (a <= 0.75) only. Null: for each cell the ln
ratios are permuted over the levels 10000 times; the share of permutations with a median slope at most the observed is the
permutation p (reported, not a rule). Reading note written before the data: a gain that exists only at a = 1 (the leak) gives a
negative slope too, so H36 alone does not say the gain survives without the leak; H34 is the test of that.

H37 (control: no headroom, no effect), chain designs (all 35, pooled over levels), norns/stock WL. Supported iff the 90% bootstrap
interval (over designs) of the mean ln ratio lies inside [ln 0.98, ln 1.02]; not supported iff the geometric mean is <= 0.97 or >= 1.03;
otherwise inconclusive. (A "not supported" would mean the Norns packer changes chain WL although chain has no headroom.)

Reported, not tested: per level and topology the norns/stock, norns/known and stock/known geometric means of CLB, WL, CPD with 90%
bootstrap intervals over designs; the fraction of the stock-to-known gap closed, (r_stock - r_norns)/(r_stock - 1), on designs with
r_stock >= 1.03 (n stated); purity of the packings against the blocks per arm and level against the null; the share of designs
whose norns `.net` equals the stock `.net` per level; W, runtimes; the placebo.
Leak check on every arm: `stock` has no word information; `norns` has the word file, whose purity `a_m` against the blocks is
reported per design (the leak is the fraction of the word file equal to the block partition); `known` has the block labels by
design (purity 1.0 by definition, reproduction checked).

## What round 6 can and cannot claim (stated before the data)

Synthetic designs from a generator; the alignment is a CONSTRUCTION (a controlled rule moving register names), not a measurement of
real HDL; the word file carries only flip-flop words (LUTs carry none); the known optimum is a clustering optimum, not a proven
WL or CPD optimum; the Norns configuration is the round-4 headline one, not tuned for partial alignment; three topologies, one
logic family (`dp` blocks of round 5). A result at a given a says what this packer does when a stated fraction of the HDL
words fall in the block, not what real HDL gives.
