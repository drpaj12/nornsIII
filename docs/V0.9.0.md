# The Norns III v0.9.0

Release notes, 2026-11-03. The Norns III is the placement experiment of Yggdrasil III (hub:
<https://github.com/drpaj12/YggdrasilIII>). v0.9.0 is the final, frozen research state of a closed path. All numbers
below come from the paper's hypothesis register and the paper (both kept locally by the author) and the plan files named next to them.

## Why 0.9 and not 1.0

The path was closed on 2026-10-31 (status: `docs/plan/STATUS.md`) and confirmed closed on 2026-11-03. The scripts and
the instruments work for the recorded experiments and reproduce the committed documents from the committed `data/`.
It is not a supported product: no packaging, no CI, runs need a local `runs/` tree that is not in the repository.
Hence 0.9.0, a frozen research record. The repository has no Python package; `VERSION` holds the number.

## What the question was

Does keeping the designer's grouping (the Odin III provenance hierarchy), or a recovered clustering (Loki III L2),
or any other cheap change to packing and placement, buy a deployable gain in speed, wire or area on VPR (VTR 9,
commit 3c9a4d23b2)? The answer is no. Nothing tried gave a gain that stands apart from noise.

## What it contains

- **Placement corpus.** Committed run records and summaries under `data/` (15 VTR Verilog designs with per-design
  records; the 35 real designs of the E10 sweep, 15 of them VTR Verilog designs). Mimir III reads the run records.
- **Known-optimum yardstick** (`gen/`, rounds 5 and 9, H38). On 33 real designs made into known-optimum yardsticks the
  standard packer needs 1.103 times the proven lower bound on CLBs and 1.265 times the wire of the known grouping
  (delay inconclusive). Over all topologies it uses 22% more wire than the known grouping, 49% more on meshes and trees.
- **Norns packer** (`packer/`): a copy of VPR's AAPack/APPack at the pinned commit with a provenance-affinity term,
  a packing census and a proposal-count option.
- **Seeding VPR's anneal** from a clustering (soft, chunk, bitwise and word seeds) with the crossover sweep and the
  checkpointing arm.
- **Placer families.** VPR's own analytic placer (AP) and simulated-annealing placer, and outside placers run on the
  same designs through VPR's flat-place interfaces: nextpnr HeAP (analytic; Gort and Anderson, FPL 2012) and
  nextpnr SA (annealing; based on arachne-pnr), on our own nextpnr "viaduct" uarch for the k6_frac_N10 grid
  (`placers/nextpnr_k6/`). DREAMPlaceFPGA and OpenPARF (electrostatic placement, elfPlace lineage) were read and
  analysed (`docs/plan/placers.md`) but not run. Results: HeAP wirelength 1.001 [0.984, 1.019] of VPR's; SA 0.947
  [0.919, 0.975] (R8a, R8b, inconclusive).
- **Packing questions** (plan files in `docs/plan/`):
  Q-N1 seed-order gap: HS0 inconclusive, answered NO for the one seed rule tried; the true seed oracle (Q-N1b) is
  possibly good in wire and area and inconclusive in speed, and a bound, not deployable.
  Q-N2 where the packer departs from the known clustering: measurement; 60% of the CLB gap is one kind of pick, an
  atom of another cluster taken while the right one was queued.
  Q-N3 ranking the right atom: measurement. Q-N3b 2-hop gain term: w=0.1 inconclusive, w=0.25 dismissed.
  Q-N4: never started.
  Q-N5 Schabas spare room: inconclusive, dismissed (more CLBs, no speed; area -10.8% to -16.8%).
  Q-N6 duplication after placement: dismissed at fixed width; the setting-a gains were selection.
- **Other tried changes**, all closed: refresh criticality while packing (k=1 inconclusive, k=8 dismissed), the
  HDL-aware packer (first gain 0.902 wirelength ratio was a leak; with realistic registers 65% to 150% more wire),
  affinity packing, hard floorplan regions, hindsight re-clustering. Geomean speed change of the later changes lies
  between -2.1% and +0.7% (paper Table `tab:tried`).
- **Register.** 88 rows: 15 useful, 10 possibly good, 27 inconclusive, 25 dismissed, 11 measurements, plus 10 untested
  ideas U1 to U10 (`docs/plan/STATUS.md`).
- **Instruments.** Heap studies, timing attribution and near-critical paths, E10 option sweep, report and paper
  generators that rebuild documents byte-identically from `data/`.

## Known limits

- Not a supported product; no package, no CI.
- Rebuilding anything upstream of `data/` needs the local `runs/` tree (archived off-machine, see `STATUS.md`) and
  the pinned VTR build; two run roots (Q-N1b, final round 9) were not found in the archive README at close.
- The Norns packer's proposal step has a measured netlist-order bias (README, "Limitations"); the fix was queued and
  not made.
- Parked, never run: a timing-aware yardstick, the Loki L3 to packer-hints bridge, seed-split duplication.
- Results are for VTR 9 at one commit, the k6_frac_N10 architecture and the designs in `data/`.
