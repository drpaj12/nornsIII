\paragraph{Round eight (other placers in the VTR flow).} Rounds one to seven use VPR's placers only. Round eight asks whether a placer from outside VTR, entering VPR through its flat-placement file (\code{--read\_flat\_place}) with no change to VTR, lands where VPR's own placers do, and reports it per family: the analytic family (VPR's analytic placer against nextpnr's HeAP) and the annealing family (VPR's simulated annealing against nextpnr's SA). It runs 15 VTR designs with six seeds, VTR~\cite{rose2012vtr} and VTR~9~\cite{elgammal2025vtr9} at commit \code{3c9a4d23b2}, and reports the held-out seeds 4 to 6. The external arms place only the logic: the adders, multipliers, RAMs and pads are fixed at the positions of VPR's analytic placer (rule H0), so every number is conditional on that. Against VPR's own placer re-reading its own placement, nextpnr HeAP is within 0.1\% in routed wirelength and 2.7\% in critical-path delay on 13 designs (WL$\times$CPD 1.028, interval 0.996 to 1.061), and nextpnr SA at a start temperature of 100 has about 5\% less routed wirelength (0.947, interval 0.919 to 0.975) with critical-path delay equal within noise (1.003); the WL$\times$CPD ratio 0.952 has an interval that includes 1. Neither external placer is timing-driven. Two of the 15 designs are ineligible. Every run of this round also writes a run record for M\'{\i}mir~III.
