\subsection{Round seven: what the data says and what it cannot say}
\label{sec:r7-discussion}
\textbf{Hindsight gap.} The arm chosen on seeds 1 to 5 is measured on seeds 6 to 10. It has a held-out gain on 1 of 15 VTR designs, is borderline on 7 and worse on 0; on 7 no arm beat the stock clustering on the selection seeds. The median held-out wirelength gap (net0 over the arm) is 1.000 over all designs (no gain = 1),
0.987 over the 8 designs with an arm, 1.050 when the borderline designs are left out (1 design) and 1.050 over the 1 design with a held-out gain; critical-path-delay gap 1.000, 0.994, 0.991, 0.991 and CLB-count gap 1.000, 1.026, 1.019, 1.019 in the same order. The in-sample medians on the selection seeds were 1.040, 1.041 and 1.026: the difference to the held-out numbers is the selection bias.
Channel width: median channel-width ratio (net0 over the arm; below 1 means the arm needs a wider channel) 0.995 held out, about 0.5\% more width, and 0.917 on the selection seeds, in sample, about 9.0\% more. On the designs without an arm the statement is about this procedure (a greedy move or swap of logic elements within
distance two from one placement), not about the packer. On the known constructions the procedure recovers a median of 0.19 of the wirelength gap on the held-out seeds, counting the 4 constructions with no gain found as 0 (12 constructions with a gap to recover; Table~\ref{tab:r7calibB} by size); over the 8 constructions where an arm was chosen the median is 0.32
(selection seeds, in sample: 0.22 pooled, 0.35 defined only). So it falls well short of the known clustering on most constructions and the real-design gap is a best-known reference, not an optimum
(the procedure keeps CLB positions of one placement fixed, leaves carry-chain CLBs alone, and picks the arm and source run by the objective it reports). A recovery above 1 means the arm beat the known clustering, which is known by construction and not optimal (wirelength: n5\_tree\_dp\_16: 1.94 held out, 1.10 on the selection seeds).
Why stock over known is 1.20 to 1.95 here against round five's headline of
about 1.22: round five's geometric mean is over all 72 designs, whose chain and random topologies have no gap (chain 1.004, random 1.019) while mesh and tree sit at 1.423 and 1.522
(\code{r5-results.tex}, topology paragraph); this round uses only mesh and tree, and the flow and the known arm are the same (\code{scripts/r5.py} \code{arm\_run} and \code{scripts/r7.py} \code{run\_arm} both use VPR's default flow and weight 100):
the stock-over-known wirelength agrees with round five's on the same designs within a factor of 0.973 to 1.078 (12 designs).

\textbf{Out-of-sample check (affinity-bias arm).} Choosing the arm on seeds 1 to 5 and evaluating stock over arm on seeds 6 to 10 finds no gain on 10 of 15 designs; on the other 5 the median wirelength gap is 0.989 (in-sample median on the same designs 1.003).

\textbf{Duplication.} The share of duplication candidates within 5\% of the critical-path delay is zero on 9 of 15 designs and the median over designs is 0.0\%; since criticality comes from the 200 reported
paths this share is a lower bound. The upper estimate of the delay all candidate hops would save is 0.0\% of the critical-path delay (median over designs, stock runs); it is an upper bound per hop, because it makes every candidate hop free.

\textbf{Mining (exploratory).} A model with HDL features, tested on held-out families (variant net\_gain, 8 designs), changes the hindsight co-location AUC on connected pairs by 0.002 (median 0.001) [-0.000, 0.005] over a baseline with net connectivity, criticality and the stock clustering's own co-location and distance, against -0.000 [-0.002, 0.001] when the HDL labels are shuffled (the interval resamples design families, not designs). The variant that keeps designs where hindsight equals the stock clustering (aff\_all) is in Table~\ref{tab:r7modelaffall} only and is not used for a headline number. On the pairs whose co-location changed between the stock and the hindsight clustering (joined against split, stock flag removed from the features) the change is 0.022 (median 0.008) over 8 designs, shuffled null -0.002. The baseline distance feature and the distance regression target use disjoint seed sets (stock: the five best seeds for the target, the other five for the baseline; \code{.net}: net0 seeds 6 to 10 for the target, 1 to 5 for the baseline), and the target placement flow of the \code{.net} target is the baseline's flow. Table~\ref{tab:r3status}'s result (same word and same module sit closer than the nets predict) re-measured here holds at the levels
same instance, depth 1, same statement, same word (negative median and at most 5\% of the shuffles at or below it); the instance chain has depth 1 in every design, so the instance levels of depth 1 and of the full path are the same relation and deeper levels do not exist. The relations tested are those in
the provenance names and the word file; module type names are not in the BLIF, so ``module'' means the instance path. Peter's bit-level hint is
answered by the rows of Table~\ref{tab:r7coef} and the ``bit'' rows of the model tables. Models are held out by design family (stereovision0 to 3, diffeq1 and 2, and the mk* modules each form a family), so a
sibling never trains the model that is tested on it.

\textbf{Limits.} Best-known, not optimal. One architecture. VTR designs only; the big VTR designs were not run. The pair universe is a sample (all
co-located pairs, a sample of connected pairs and a uniform sample), the evaluation set is the two unbiased samples, and the 200 reported timing paths bound
every criticality used. The null arm (net0 with permuted legal slots through the same selection rule) was not run, so the part of the held-out gain that a random legal rearrangement plus selection would give is not measured; the held-out seeds remove the selection bias on the seeds but not a bias of the greedy rule itself. The families for the model leave-out are decided from the Verilog: boundtop and raygentop, two designs of one ray-tracing system, share no module name and only two non-trivial source lines, so they are separate families. Nothing here is a hypothesis test with a status; the associations are the data's, with their nulls.
