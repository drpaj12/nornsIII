\subsection{Round three results}
\label{sec:r3-results}

\textbf{RQ-N8, does packing dilute structure?} H8 is supported: over the 15 stock packings the median word purity
(plurality share) is 0.63 against 0.20 for a random grouping of the same atoms (higher on 15 of 15), and the median
split factor is 4.00 (above 1 on 12 of 15). The median module purity is 0.99, so module affinity has little to gain on most designs. Of the logic atoms, 65.8\% carry a word (median); 34.2\% lack one (front end: ABC's cone provenance, mixed
spans 33.8\% and no match 0.4\%) and a further 20.7\% carry a word but sit in a CLB where their word is not the
plurality word or fails the 1/3 rule (packing).

\textbf{RQ-N10, I/O words.} H10a is supported: the median LUT word coverage rises from 31.8\% to 53.9\% with io-words
(median gain 24.1\%, positive on 15 of 15 designs).

\textbf{Seed noise.} The stock packer's routed wirelength varies across the three VPR seeds with a median coefficient of variation
2.4\% (maximum 9.7\%, n=15 designs), which is the floor under any wirelength difference below a few percent.

\textbf{module affinity.} H9a is inconclusive (pilot-sensitive): the module-level purity moves by 0.000 (median, n=15; 0.035 without the pilot designs) with a median change of -1.6\% in logic CLBs (largest 13.3\%). H9b is inconclusive (pilot-sensitive): with the analytic start the routed wirelength is 1.051 of the stock packer's (better on 3 of 15; 90\% interval 1.020 to 1.082; 1.069 without the pilot designs, status there supported (higher WL)). H9c is not supported: with the word seed it is 1.064 (better on 2 of 15).

\textbf{word affinity.} H9a is supported: the word-level purity moves by 0.111 (median, n=15; 0.120 without the pilot designs) with a median change of 0.0\% in logic CLBs (largest 13.3\%). H9b is inconclusive (pilot-sensitive): with the analytic start the routed wirelength is 1.032 of the stock packer's (better on 4 of 15; 90\% interval 1.006 to 1.062; 1.053 without the pilot designs, status there supported (higher WL)). H9c is not supported: with the word seed it is 1.039 (better on 4 of 15).

\textbf{word+io affinity.} H9a is supported: the word+io-level purity moves by 0.104 (median, n=15; 0.124 without the pilot designs) with a median change of 0.0\% in logic CLBs (largest 13.3\%). H9b is inconclusive (pilot-sensitive): with the analytic start the routed wirelength is 1.030 of the stock packer's (better on 4 of 15; 90\% interval 1.005 to 1.057; 1.050 without the pilot designs, status there supported (higher WL)). H9c is not supported: with the word seed it is 1.039 (better on 5 of 15).

\textbf{I/O words in the affinity (H10b).} not supported: word+io over word is 0.998 (better on 4 of 15, $p=1.000$).
