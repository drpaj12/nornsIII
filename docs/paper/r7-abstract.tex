\paragraph{Round seven (data first).} Rounds one to six each tested an idea. Round seven tests none: it runs 1660 VPR placements (15 VTR designs and 12 constructions whose block clustering is known by construction) and reports what the data shows. All runs use VTR~\cite{rose2012vtr}, VTR~9~\cite{elgammal2025vtr9}, commit \code{3c9a4d23b2}. The hindsight clustering is the stock clustering with logic elements moved or swapped between neighbouring CLBs according to where the best stock placement put them; it is written as a VPR \code{.net} and run with packing off, so VPR builds exactly that clustering. The arm is chosen on seeds 1 to 5 and measured on the held-out seeds 6 to 10. On the held-out seeds the arm has an objective interval above 1 on 1 of 15 VTR designs (7 borderline, 0 worse) and no gain was found on 7. The held-out wirelength gap (stock clustering over the arm) has median 1.000 over all 15 designs (no gain found counted as 1) and 0.987 over the 8 designs where an arm was chosen; critical-path-delay gap 1.000 and 0.994, CLB-count gap 1.000 and 1.026. Median channel-width ratio (net0 over the arm; below 1 means the arm needs a wider channel) 0.995 held out, about 0.5\% more width, and 0.917 on the selection seeds, in sample, about 9.0\% more. On the selection seeds, in sample, the median wirelength gap over the same 8 designs is 1.040. On the known constructions the procedure recovers a median of 0.19 of the wirelength gap held out (no gain counted as 0; 0.32 over the 8 constructions where it found one). The earlier affinity-bias arm, tested out of sample (arm chosen on seeds 1 to 5, evaluated on 6 to 10), finds no gain on 10 of 15 designs and a median wirelength gap of 0.989 on the others. On held-out design families, HDL features change the AUC of predicting which pairs hindsight co-locates by 0.003 (shuffled-label null -0.000), so in this data they add almost nothing beyond net structure and the stock clustering. Everything after the gap is exploratory and carries a shuffled-label null. The gap is a best-known bound made by selection, not an optimum.
