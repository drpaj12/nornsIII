\paragraph{Round seven (data first).} Rounds one to six each tested an idea. Round seven tests none: it runs 889 VPR placements (10 VTR designs and 12 known-optimum constructions, 10 seeds, stock and hindsight clusterings) and reports what the data shows. The hindsight clustering re-packs the atoms where the best placement put them, and the same placer and router, seed for seed, give a median wirelength gap of 1.000, critical-path-delay gap of 1.000 and CLB-count gap of 1.000 (stock over hindsight, median over designs). Everything after the gap is exploratory and carries a shuffled-label null. The gap is a best-known bound made by selection, not an optimum.
