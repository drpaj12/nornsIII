\paragraph{Round seven (data first).} Rounds one to six each tested an idea. Round seven tests none: it runs 1430 VPR placements (15 VTR designs and 12 known-optimum constructions, ten seeds) and reports what the data shows. The hindsight clustering is the stock clustering with logic elements moved or swapped between neighbouring CLBs according to where the best stock placement put them; it is written as a VPR \code{.net} and run with packing off, so VPR builds exactly that clustering. The procedure finds a gain on 8 of 15 VTR designs; on those, the stock clustering over the hindsight clustering, seed for seed in sample (the arm is chosen on the same seeds), has a median wirelength gap of 1.040, critical-path-delay gap of 1.041 and CLB-count gap of 1.026. On the other 7 designs no gain was found. Everything after the gap is exploratory and carries a shuffled-label null. The gap is a best-known bound made by selection, not an optimum.
