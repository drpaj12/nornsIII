\subsection{Round seven results}

\label{sec:r7-results}

All VPR runs of this round use VTR~\cite{rose2012vtr} and VTR~9~\cite{elgammal2025vtr9} at commit \code{3c9a4d23b2}. The corpus has 980 completed analytical-placement VPR runs (0 failed) in 1.73~h of VPR time and 680 runs of the \code{.net} arms (0 failed, 435 on the selection seeds, 245 on the held-out seeds, of which 15 are the n5\_mesh\_dp\_16 probe run before the protocol amendment) in 1.06~h; Table~\ref{tab:r7runs} counts the runs per arm and seeds and Table~\ref{tab:r7corpus} lists the designs. Table~\ref{tab:r7gap} and Figure~\ref{fig:r7gap} give the hindsight gap of the \code{.net} arm on the held-out seeds (the headline) next to the selection seeds: the arm has a held-out gain on 1 of 15 designs (blob\_merge), is borderline on 7 and worse on 0, and no arm beat the stock clustering on 7. The median held-out wirelength gap is 1.000 over all designs (no gain found = 1), 0.987 over the designs with an arm and 1.050 over the designs with a held-out gain only (the last is selected on the held-out result and is the most optimistic); on the selection seeds, in sample, the median over the designs with an arm was 1.040. Channel width: median channel-width ratio (net0 over the arm; below 1 means the arm needs a wider channel) 0.995 held out, about 0.5\% more width, and 0.917 on the selection seeds, in sample, about 9.0\% more. The equivalence check (\code{scripts/r7netcheck.py}) ran on all 114 written \code{.net} files (388883 atoms; the stock files are included): 114 of 114 files pass; 0 atoms differ from the BLIF in their input nets, 0 from the stock \code{.net}, 0 nets read outside their CLB lack an output pin, 0 CLB input pins carry a net that is undriven where the stock file drives it (compared per pin, not by count; the stock files themselves have 2 such pins, in 2 designs). The checker has its own parser and compares the pin-index to net lists with the stock file. The layout of the \code{clbouts} output pins is validated by the stock \code{.net} files that VPR wrote passing the same check, and by VPR reading every written file. Table~\ref{tab:r7gapaff} gives the affinity-bias arm, Tables~\ref{tab:r7calib} and~\ref{tab:r7calibB} the calibration against the known clustering, Table~\ref{tab:r7dup} the duplication counts, and Tables~\ref{tab:r7xi} to~\ref{tab:r7coef} with Figures~\ref{fig:r7classes} and~\ref{fig:r7dist} the first mining. All mining is exploratory.
