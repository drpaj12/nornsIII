
\subsection{Round six: results}
\label{sec:r6-results}
Table~\ref{tab:r6status} gives every hypothesis with its status and deciding number. Table~\ref{tab:r6level} gives, per alignment level, the Norns / stock wirelength ratio (geometric mean over designs, 90\% bootstrap interval), the placebo and the measured alignment; Table~\ref{tab:r6known} the gap to the known clustering. At no non-leaky alignment tested (a in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75}) does the literal H34 rule support a wirelength gain of HDL-aware packing over stock VPR (statuses: a=0 not supported, a=0.25 not supported, a=0.5 not supported, a=0.75 not supported); with the leak (a=1) the wirelength ratio is 0.669 (supported); it hurts (WL at least 2\% worse, Holm) at a in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75}.
