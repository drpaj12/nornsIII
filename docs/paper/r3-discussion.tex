\subsection{Round three discussion}

\label{sec:r3-discussion}



Packing does dilute word structure (H8 supported): a word's atoms are spread over about 4.00 times the CLBs they would need, and the purity of the stock CLBs (0.63) is well above a random grouping (0.20) but far from word-pure. Of the logic atoms, 34.2\% have no word at all (the front end) and a further 20.7\% are word atoms packed away from their word (packing); these are two separate per-design medians, and the median front-end loss is larger than the median packing loss.

I/O words are a cheap way to recover part of the front-end loss (H10a supported: LUT word coverage 31.8\% to 53.9\%).

The Norns packer does what it was built to do: with the word affinity the word purity rises by 0.111 at a median CLB change of 0.0\% (H9a supported; module inconclusive (pilot-sensitive), word+io supported). The module source has almost nothing to act on, because the designs are close to one module each (median module purity 0.99).

Purer clusters did not give shorter wires. With the analytic start the word-affinity packing routes at 1.032 of the stock packer's wirelength (better on 4 of 15 designs, 90\% interval 1.006 to 1.062), so H9b is inconclusive (pilot-sensitive): the sign is positive (worse) on every source, the two-sided test reaches the 5\% level only without the four pilot designs, and the status is therefore not stated as a change. With the round-two word seed rebuilt on the new packing the ratio is 1.039 (H9c not supported): giving the round-two seed word-pure CLBs does not rescue it. The packing time grows by a factor 1.125 (word) and 1.318 (module). The seed noise (median coefficient of variation 2.4\%, up to 9.7\%) is of the same order as the effect, which is why the statistics use the design as the unit with three seeds each.

These runs do not remove the start-temperature confound of round two for the seeded arms, because a seeded start still carries its own start temperature; the analytic-start arm (H9b) does not have it, since the packing is the only difference.

In the median design both relations sit closer than their direct nets alone predict (stratified distance difference
-0.176 for same-word pairs, -0.188 for same-module pairs), but not on every design: the same-word difference is positive on
3 designs (\code{boundtop}, \code{raygentop}, \code{stereovision2}) and the same-module difference on 1 designs (\code{blob\_merge}). H11 is inconclusive under the protocol text, because its rule needs both
relations to pass: same word, n=12, status inconclusive (negative in two of three seeds on 9 designs, 12 needed);
same module, n=11, status inconclusive (10 designs, 12 needed). Two deviations of the analysis from the protocol text are reported
beside it, not instead of it: the unregistered scaling of the count to $\lceil 12n/15\rceil$ designs, which the first analysis used
(\code{r3stats.py}), gives word inconclusive (n=12), module supported (n=11), overall inconclusive; the amendment \code{d805789}, which excludes a design from a relation when 95\% or more of its
sampled pairs are related, for the word relation as well as the module relation, gives word inconclusive (n=12), module inconclusive (n=11), overall inconclusive. Both are recorded in the entry of
2026-10-25 of \code{docs/plan/round3.md} and in the Method. The neighbours are only partly stable: the mean Jaccard of
each CLB's four nearest neighbours between VPR seeds is 0.267, against 0.058 for a permuted placement.


Round three leaves open the effort curve (N3), larger designs (N5), region looseness (N6), a seed-selector change in the Norns packer, and whether a different gain scale (the grid ended at the largest beta of the pilot list; the pilot did not discriminate the betas, so the grid is not informed by it) changes the sign of H9b. The packing time ratios of Stage 4 compare two builds and the proposal step has the counting and ordering limits described in the Method, both to be fixed in round 4.
