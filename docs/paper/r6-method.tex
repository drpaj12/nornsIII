
\subsection{Round six: method}
\label{sec:r6-method}
\paragraph{Question N Q3.1 on the yardstick of N Q1.6.} As the alignment $a$ between the HDL words and the optimal blocks falls from 1 to 0, how does the gain of the Norns HDL-aware packer over the stock VPR packer in wirelength, CLB count and critical-path delay change, and does it ever do worse? Round five could not say: in its \code{dp} designs the word file equals the block partition.
\paragraph{Designs.} The logic of a round-six design is exactly the round-five \code{dp} design of the same topology and size (same blocks, functions, wiring and fixed I/O; each block fills one CLB). Only the HDL declaration of the registers changes: inside a block the registers and ports are scalars, and the top module declares $B$ vectors \code{w<j>[9:0]} that are the HDL words. At alignment $a$, a fraction $1-a$ of each block's flip-flops is displaced and dealt, ordered by local bit then block, into the displaced slots, so a word holds the same local bit of consecutive blocks (a bit-slice). At $a=1$ the words are the blocks (the leak, the reference); at $a=0$ every word holds ten flip-flops of ten blocks and a block's flip-flops sit in ten words. Levels $a\in\{0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1\}$, topologies mesh, tree and chain, $B\in\{16,\dots,1024\}$ (seven sizes): 105 designs. The alignment is measured after synthesis as the purity of the word file against the blocks. Arms: \code{stock}, \code{norns} (round-four headline configuration, fed the word file as written) and \code{known} (block membership as affinity, $\beta=100$), three VPR seeds each. Primary metric of every hypothesis is wirelength; CLB count and delay are secondary. The placebo is the ratio of stock at level $a$ to stock at $a=1$ of the same cell: the same logic with different register names.
Rules, thresholds and statuses are in the protocol (\code{docs/plan/round6.md}, SHA \code{0ffcfc1}) and its dated entries; the paper repeats none of them. Excluded by the rule fixed in advance: none.
